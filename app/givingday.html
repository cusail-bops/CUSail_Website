Donation Page

Help Us Out!

Designing a world-class autonomous sailboat is fun, but challenging work.

Consider donating to help us strive further.

We turn theory into reality and apply our learning as we convert CAD designs into carbon fiber lay-ups and bytes into sophisticated event algorithms.

This year, however, we hope to not only come first in competition, but we’re aiming even higher: We want to autonomously sail across the Atlantic Ocean. Your help can make our dreams possible!

Donation Goals

$5: Cellular Telemetry

A $5 donation allows us to connect our boat to cellular service for telemetry and command sending

$10: Boat Harnessing & Wiring

A $10 donation helps cover the cost of all the harnessing and wiring within our boat

$20: Boat Computer & Batteries

A $20 donation covers the cost of our boat computer and batteries

$50: Sail & Ballast Hardware

A $50 donation covers the cost of our boat's hardware, such as our sail and ballast

$100: Send a Member to Competition

A $100 donation can fund the travel, food, and lodging expenses to send a member to our annual competition!

$500: GPS & IMU Sensors

A $500 donation will allow us to buy top-of-the-line GPS and IMU sensors, which will improve our autonomous algorithms!
